## Design Context

### Users
FlowDay supports daily study and work planning through schedules, tasks, projects, and workflows. The current iteration targets the owner's desktop use and browser preview.

### Brand Personality
The owner selected concept A: simple, quiet, and calm. Preserve the established blue-and-white visual identity.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light surfaces, muted indigo accents, restrained borders, clear Chinese typography, and comfortable spacing. Quick capture should span the available content width. Dropdowns should use consistent floating menus, a soft selected state, and a clear checkmark. Respect existing dark mode and narrow-window layouts.

### Design Principles
- Give primary inputs enough room to read and type comfortably.
- Keep controls and menus visually consistent across modules.
- Preserve keyboard interaction and accessible labels.
- Adapt to window width without shrinking text or touch targets excessively.
- Keep this iteration focused on quick capture and dropdowns.
